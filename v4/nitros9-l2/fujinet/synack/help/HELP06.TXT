{ WHERE THE MONEY IS                6/11 }

Exploits don't pay. Crypto does.
You get paid when a hive you lead is
absorbed, or when the game ends.

FOUNDING is worth little by itself (one
free crypto). It gets you in first, at
the lowest price.

THE PAYDAY: lead a small hive next to a
bigger one, then run the exploit that
joins them. Only the absorbed hive pays
ROOT and SUDO: 10x and 5x its price.

SWAP: 2 absorbed for 1 absorber's crypto
to get into a big hive for no \\\.

CASH is tight: Buy up to 3 crypto/turn,
and nothing pays until a hivejack or the
end. An early bonus buys the next ROOT.
